# Personal Storefront Pivot

On September 4, Murad said he has not streamed since the second day and wants AI with Murda to become a sales funnel where people learn about him and buy scripts and folders.

This direction supersedes the homepage rule that the scoreboard is the main character. The historical challenge and prior project records are preserved; the sales journey should not require ongoing streams, a countdown, or customer participation in a challenge.

Discovery and proposed implementation are in `../SALES-FUNNEL-REDESIGN.md`. Production was inspected read-only. No site deployment or pricing change was made in this discovery pass.

Verified: 17 paid catalog products plus one free sampler exist; all 18 product folders passed the structural verifier. Standalone checkout is not wired. The starter and member pages exist. The Toolkit checkout currently requires both setup and recurring updates despite optional-update wording in some marketing copy.

Preserve Render hosting, Backbone-only Stripe payments, Supabase member access, existing purchases, and immutable entitlement keys. Proposed featured selection and subscription changes still need to be made concrete during implementation; do not treat them as approved new pricing.
