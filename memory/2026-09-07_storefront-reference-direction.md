# Product-First Storefront Direction

After reviewing the first storefront preview, Murad asked for plain-English explanations of free and paid contents, then supplied Flux Growth Agency and AI for Savages as clearer reference sites. He requested research into these and other stores.

Research is saved in `../STOREFRONT-REFERENCE-STUDY.md`, including Thomas Frank and Refactoring UI. The proposed next iteration leads with recognizable jobs and real output previews, clearly distinguishes free downloads, paid packages, and included tools, and presents bundles after people understand the individual resources.

This research turn did not change the site, pricing, access, package contents, or free allocation. Keep the existing sampler as one free pack with three parts; do not silently give away paid products or invent standalone checkout. No competitor conversion or performance claims were verified.

Preserve the existing local implementation, Backbone-only Stripe, Supabase access, and $47/$97 pricing. Toolkit remains $327 today then $30/month under current checkout. Visual labels and page organization can improve without changing these commercial terms.
