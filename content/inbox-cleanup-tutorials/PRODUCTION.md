## Production rules

- Film the actual shipped tool. No generated terminal windows, fake login success,
  invented settings or fabricated before/after results.
- Label practice mail as fictional. It proves the walkthrough, not Gmail/iCloud compatibility.
- Real-provider acceptance requires an owner-authorized disposable, backed-up inbox.
  Owner enters credentials off camera, outside the AI assistant's session.
- Keep personal mail, passwords, accounts.json, raw scans, logs and other projects off screen.
- Do not clone Murad's voice or synthesize an endorsement without his explicit consent.
  Use a built-in narration voice or Murad's own recording initially.
- Favor readable screen zooms and captions over animation. No unlicensed music.
- Both tutorials disclose the Delete default, manual unsubscribes, limited Trash
  retention, and lack of built-in backup/undo. No automatic-unsubscribe claims.

## Tutorial 1: Without AI

Target: 3-4 minutes with setup. Record the real menu or `python3 -B start.py --demo`.
Sample-only inputs: `1`, `d`, `a`, `f`, `Receipts`, `k`, `y`, `y`, `MOVE 6`.
Never reuse that input sequence against a real mailbox.

Narration:

"Let's practice first. These eight messages are made up. This demonstration
cannot change your real mail. A real scan reads inbox headers, not message bodies
or attachments. Starting with a hundred messages keeps the first run manageable.

Now we review each sender. I'll move these sample offers to Trash. Delete is the
default: pressing Enter chooses it. This is true even for personal mail, so do
not repeatedly press Enter without reading. Keep, Archive, Folder, and the manual
unsubscribe option are also available.

I'll archive the lessons, put the receipt in a Receipts folder, and keep Alex's
message. The starred receipt stays in the inbox. If I stop now, only my choices
have been saved. No messages have moved.

Next is the preview. Three offers go to Trash, two lessons go to Archive, and one
receipt goes to my named folder. Read every sender, destination and count. A
preview is not permission to run. I choose to continue and then type the exact
confirmation shown by the tool. Six sample messages move. Two stay: Alex's mail
and the starred receipt. This is a simulated result, not a live mailbox test.

For your own account, back up important mail and choose the without-AI route.
Enter an app password only in the local hidden prompt. Start small and inspect
the result in your mail app. Trash can empty automatically under your provider's
rules. This kit does not make a backup or offer automatic undo.

Unsubscribing remains manual. The kit makes a sender checklist; finish those
unsubscribes through your mail app's controls. It does not open links or send
unsubscribe requests. Revoke the app password with your provider when finished."

## Tutorial 2: With AI

Target: 4-5 minutes with setup. Film an actual assistant working from a fresh
fictional sender report and the real local suggestion-review flow. Do not fake
the response. Show the report, assistant's reasoning, JSON file and human review.

Narration:

"This route adds your assistant's advice. The kit is free, but AI subscriptions
or usage charges are separate. Account setup still happens locally. Your
assistant does not need your app password.

The menu's Prepare a sender report option makes a small read-only scan. Its AI
report contains sender addresses, counts and saved choices. It leaves out
subjects, message bodies, display names and passwords. Addresses are still
private information. Inspect this specific file before sharing it with your AI
provider. Never upload the whole used kit or data folder. Our demonstration uses
fictional addresses only.

Give your assistant the included guide, suggestions format and that report.
Tell it what must stay: family, bills, receipts and account-security notices,
for example. It should ask about uncertain senders, not assume volume means junk.

The assistant returns suggestions, not an execution plan. Choose Review my AI's
suggestions in the local menu, select the same account, and enter that file's
path. The tool checks that it matches your scan. If it doesn't, export a fresh
report; don't modify the tool to bypass the check.

Each suggestion appears beside a sender. You still choose the action. Important:
Enter means Delete, not accept the AI recommendation. If the assistant says Keep
and you agree, type k. If one sender mixes offers with important mail, choose Keep
and handle individual messages in your mail app.

Then read the preview and complete the final typed confirmation yourself. The
assistant should not type it, change rules or execute cleanup. A response saying
Done does not mean your inbox was cleaned.

Inspect the result in your mail app. Unsubscribes remain manual. Back up important
mail first, remember Trash retention is limited, and revoke the app password
when finished. You get help reviewing, while final decisions stay yours."
